## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the understated exclusivity of bespoke private travel, tailored for discerning, high-net-worth voyagers seeking effortless composure and curated refinement. The aesthetic is anchored in an opulent midnight atmosphere, marrying the quiet architectural poise of obsidian monoliths with the delicate warmth of spun champagne gold.

Interactions evoke the tactility of a private salon: unhurried, frictionless, and precise. The visual language balances luxury minimalism with subtle dark glassmorphism—employing selective illumination, hairline filigree borders, and luminous metallic highlights rather than loud ornamentation. 

By unifying all typography under a singular, high-performance grotesque sans-serif, the system achieves an international modernist poise: razor-sharp, sleek, legible across every global script, and uncompromisingly contemporary. The emotional response is one of total serenity, discretion, and timeless prestige.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep obsidian plane upon which metallic golden accents capture light like warm gilding in low ambient evening glow.

- **Primary (`#d4a017` / Rich Champagne Gold):** Reserved for premier focal points, active state highlights, refined timeline milestones, and luxury badge accents.
- **Secondary (`#fabc4d` / Polished Brass):** Drives directional gradients, secondary interactions, and warm hover illuminations.
- **Tertiary (`#d1c6a5` / Pale Gold Leaf):** An ethereal, ultra-light metallic tint utilized for elevated titles, metallic text gradients, and shimmering hairline borders.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#0a0b0e` Void & `#121316` Deep Obsidian):** Forms the layered baseline of all views. Surface layers rise subtly through container tiers (`#1b1b1f` up to `#343538`) and translucent charcoal glass overlays.
- **Border Spectrum:** Deliberately nuanced, blending from `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.18)` along top and light-facing edges down to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` along neutral baselines to emulate physically chamfered metallic edges.

## Typography

The typography is unified across all viewports, components, headings, and body surfaces using **Noto Sans**. This produces a sleek, international, modernist aesthetic with exceptional universal language support and razor-sharp digital precision against deep obsidian surfaces.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy:** Rendered in light and regular weights (`300` to `500`) with precise negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to create an architectural, gallery-grade confidence without ornamental flourishes. Large hero headlines balance generous line height with sleek geometric proportion.
- **Body Elements:** Standardized in neutral weights with comfortable reading rhythm, maximizing legibility on dark glassmorphic tiers and backlit displays.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Horology Tags:** Capitalized treatments with wide tracked spacing (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) evoke the crisp engraving on high-end horological dials, flight manifests, and private banking portals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, architectural fluid grid that emphasizes generous negative space, intentional asymmetry, and rigid structural alignment.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid, max content envelope of `1360px`, `4rem` outer margins, `2rem` gutters. Accommodates dual-pane itinerary maps alongside chronological detail sheets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Collapses side inspection panels into floating bottom-anchored sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Timeline elements transition from horizontal cards to a continuous vertical gilded thread.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) separate major journey chapters, preventing density fatigue and giving each stage of travel the weight of an exhibition piece.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is orchestrated using darkness tiers and frosted obsidian glass, simulating deep-space layers with selective gold illumination:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure foundation `#0a0b0e`. Completely non-reflective, absorbent dark plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Shells):** `rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.65)` layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(140%)`. Outlined with a top-weighted gradient border: `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.18)` bleeding downward into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** Surface shifts to `rgba(26, 30, 40, 0.85)` with ambient shadow `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 0 20px 1px rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.08)`. Border shifts dynamically to `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** `#121316` with 95% opacity, blur radius `32px`, bound by a razor-thin champagne glow `0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.25)` and deep drop shadow `0 30px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a structured, architectural geometry (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are subtly softened rather than overly rounded, reflecting precision craftsmanship and industrial poise rather than casual consumer app ergonomics.

- **Standard Elements (Cards, Panels, Sheets):** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) corner radii for tailored, architectural presence.
- **Compact Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.25rem` (`rounded`) corner radii for crisp tactile feedback.
- **Floating Modals & Hero Canvas Strips:** `0.75rem` (`rounded-xl`) max roundedness, ensuring structural discipline is strictly preserved.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Obsidian / Metallic Hover):** Resting state is an understated monolith: solid `#121316` with a micro-border `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.3)` and Pale Gold leaf typography (`#f3e7c4`, Noto Sans `label-lg`). On hover, transitions effortlessly (300ms cubic-bezier) to a lustrous diagonal metallic gradient `linear-gradient(135deg, #fabc4d 0%, #d4a017 50%, #bd8718 100%)`, text shifting to obsidian black (`#0a0b0e`) with an elevated golden ambient aura.
- **Secondary Ghost:** Transparent body, framed in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, typography in `#f3e7c4`. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.08)` and border illuminates to `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.35)`.

### Cards & Itinerary Modules
- Built with Level 1 glassmorphism: charcoal glass (`rgba(18, 20, 26, 0.7)`), 20px blur, and hairline warm border.
- Headings set in Noto Sans `headline-sm` with subtle metallic gradient fills. Hover states initiate a subtle 2px vertical lift, intensification of the golden top border (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.4)`), and a soft inner ambient bloom.

### Chips & Badges
- Flight classes, booking confirmations, and timing indicators use ultra-thin capsule outlines in `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.2)` with background fills of `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.06)`. Labels feature uppercase Noto Sans `label-sm` tracking with `#f3e7c4`.

### Lists & Timeline Trackers
- Connected vertically by a continuous hairline gold path (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.2)`).
- Waypoints are represented by dual-ring nodes: outer glow ring in dark obsidian, inner pinpoint in solid `#d4a017`. Section indicators and timestamps utilize Noto Sans `label-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base state: Obsidian box `#121316` with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` border.
- Checked state: Border and core filled with `#d4a017`, checkmark rendered in `#0a0b0e`.

### Input Fields
- Dark glass surface (`rgba(14, 16, 21, 0.8)`), border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`, value text in Noto Sans `body-md` (`#e3e2e6`).
- Focused state: Border transitions smoothly to `rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.5)` with a discreet `0 0 12px rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.15)` outer glow. Placeholder text in muted platinum slate (`#9b8f7a`).

### Specialized Itinerary Elements
- **Concierge Drawer:** Persistent dark obsidian floating bar pinned to the viewport bottom with quick-access flight telemetry, private transfer statuses, and direct curator messaging.
- **Chronology Header:** Sticky month/day tracker rendered in sleek, light-weight Noto Sans with gold leaf foil gradient fill text mask.
